English

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Auto Clicker: Tap & Swipe (package com.howard.autoclicker, "the app") handles data. The app is developed and published by Zhang Hang (张航) ("we"). Contact: zhanghang2357@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app works fully offline. It does not request the internet permission and never sends data off your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party libraries that collect data.
  • We do not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal or sensitive user data.

Accessibility Service (AccessibilityService API)

Auto Clicker: Tap & Swipe is an automation tool, not an accessibility tool. It uses the Android Accessibility Service API only to perform the taps and swipes that you set up yourself:

  • Performing gestures: the service dispatches taps and swipes at the positions you placed or recorded, and only after you press Play on the floating bar.
  • Recording: only while you are recording, the app notes where and for how long you touch the screen (coordinates and timing) so it can replay the same gestures. This information is saved as part of your script on your device.
  • No screen reading: the service is configured without canRetrieveWindowContent and does not subscribe to accessibility events. It cannot read text, passwords, messages or the content of other apps.

Before you are sent to the system Accessibility settings, the app shows an in-app disclosure that explains this use and asks for your consent. If you decline, the rest of the app (editing scripts and settings) keeps working. You can turn the service off at any time in the system Accessibility settings.

Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used only to show the floating control bar, the numbered target markers and the recording layer on top of other apps.

Data stored on your device

  • Click settings (interval, repeat count, jitter, playback timing), the current list of actions (tap/swipe positions and timing) and your saved scripts are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your in-app consent to the accessibility disclosure and your chosen app language are stored locally.
  • None of this data is sent to us or to any third party.

Backup

If Android backup (for example Google backup) is turned on for your device, your settings, actions and scripts may be included in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account and is end-to-end encrypted where supported by your device. We have no access to it. The accessibility consent record is excluded from backup, so you are asked again after restoring to a new device.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete scripts in the app. Clearing the app's storage (Settings › Apps › Auto Clicker › Storage › Clear storage) or uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Security

All data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to protect.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for a general adult audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address (https://howard-hang.github.io/autoclicker-privacy/). If the way the app uses the Accessibility Service changes materially, the app will ask for your consent again.

Contact

Questions about this policy: zhanghang2357@gmail.com.

本隐私政策说明安卓应用 自动点击：点按与滑动（Auto Clicker: Tap & Swipe）（包名 com.howard.autoclicker，以下称「本应用」）如何处理数据。本应用由 张航（以下称「我们」）开发和发布。联系方式：zhanghang2357@gmail.com。

概要

  • 本应用完全离线运行，不申请联网权限，数据不会离开你的设备。
  • 没有账号、广告、统计或崩溃上报 SDK，也没有收集数据的第三方库。
  • 我们不收集、不出售、不分享、不转移任何个人数据或敏感数据。

无障碍服务（AccessibilityService API）

本应用是自动化工具，不是无障碍辅助工具。它使用 Android 无障碍服务 API，只用来执行你自己设置的点击和滑动：

  • 执行手势：在你摆放或录制的位置点击、滑动，而且只在你点了悬浮条上的播放之后。
  • 录制：只在录制期间，记录你在屏幕上触摸的位置和时长（坐标和时间），用来原样回放。这些信息作为方案的一部分保存在你的设备上。
  • 不读取屏幕：服务没有开启 canRetrieveWindowContent，也不订阅无障碍事件，读不到屏幕上的文字、密码、消息或其他应用的内容。

跳转到系统无障碍设置之前，本应用会先在应用内显示用途说明并请你同意。你不同意时，应用的其它功能（编辑方案和设置）照常可用。你可以随时在系统无障碍设置里关闭本服务。

显示在其他应用上层（SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW）

只用来在其他应用上层显示悬浮控制条、编号目标标记和录制层。

保存在设备上的数据

  • 点击参数（间隔、轮数、抖动、按录制节奏）、当前动作列表（点击/滑动的位置和时长）以及你保存的方案，存放在本应用的私有存储中。
  • 你在应用内对无障碍用途说明的同意记录、你选择的应用语言，也保存在本机。
  • 这些数据都不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

备份

如果你的设备开启了 Android 备份（例如 Google 备份），参数、动作和方案可能包含在你自己的设备备份中，由 Google 在你的 Google 账号下处理（设备支持时为端到端加密）。我们无法访问。无障碍同意记录不参与备份，换到新设备恢复后会再次请你确认。

保留与删除

数据会一直保存在你的设备上，直到你删除。你可以在应用内删除方案；清除应用存储（设置 › 应用 › 自动点击 › 存储 › 清除存储）或卸载应用会删除它的全部数据。

安全

所有数据都保存在本应用的私有存储中，其他应用无法读取。由于不传输任何数据，不存在传输中的数据。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，面向一般成年用户。

变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新上面的生效日期，并在同一地址（https://howard-hang.github.io/autoclicker-privacy/）发布新版本。如果本应用使用无障碍服务的方式发生实质变化，会重新请你同意。
