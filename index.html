English

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Auto Clicker: Tap & Swipe (package com.howard.autoclicker, "the app") handles data. The app is developed and published by Zhang Hang (张航) ("we"). Contact: zhanghang2357@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There are no accounts and no ads. We do not collect personal information, and we never sell data or use it for advertising.
  • Your taps, swipes, recordings and scripts stay on your device. Nothing the Accessibility Service handles is ever collected or sent.
  • To understand which features are used and to fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous usage events and crash reports (details below). You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data.

Accessibility Service (AccessibilityService API)

Auto Clicker: Tap & Swipe is an automation tool, not an accessibility tool. It uses the Android Accessibility Service API only to perform the taps and swipes that you set up yourself:

  • Performing gestures: the service dispatches taps and swipes at the positions you placed or recorded, and only after you press Play on the floating bar.
  • Recording: only while you are recording, the app notes where and for how long you touch the screen (coordinates and timing) so it can replay the same gestures. This information is saved as part of your script on your device and is never uploaded.
  • No screen reading: the service is configured without canRetrieveWindowContent and does not subscribe to accessibility events. It cannot read text, passwords, messages or the content of other apps.

Before you are sent to the system Accessibility settings, the app shows an in-app disclosure that explains this use and asks for your consent. If you decline, the rest of the app (editing scripts and settings) keeps working. You can turn the service off at any time in the system Accessibility settings.

Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used only to show the floating control bar, the numbered target markers and the recording layer on top of other apps.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports (Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, which act as our service provider.

What is collected

  • App-usage events, for example: the floating bar was shown, playback started or stopped, a recording was saved, a script was saved, selected or deleted, the app language or a setting was changed, a permission was granted. Events only carry counts in ranges (such as "2-5 actions"), durations in ranges, and fixed options (such as tap/swipe, the stop reason, the name of the setting that changed, the chosen app language). The app language is also stored as a user property.
  • Crash logs (stack traces and the app state at the time of the crash) and diagnostics.
  • Device information: device model, Android version, app version and language/region settings.
  • Approximate location: country and city, which Google Analytics derives from your IP address. The app does not request location permission and never collects precise (GPS) location; the IP address itself is used only to derive this and is not stored by Google Analytics.
  • The Firebase installation ID, a random identifier for this installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account and is reset when you clear the app's storage or reinstall it.

What is never collected

  • Personal information such as your name, email, phone number or accounts.
  • Precise (GPS) location.
  • Anything from the Accessibility Service: screen content, text, tap or swipe positions, gesture paths.
  • Which other apps you use or automate (package names), and the names you give your scripts.
  • The Advertising ID (the AD_ID permission is removed from the app, and advertising ID, SSAID collection and ad personalization signals are turned off).

How it is used

Only to understand how features are used, to improve the app and to find and fix crashes. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than Google acting as our service provider. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Your choice

Usage data sharing is on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, the app sends neither usage events nor crash reports. Debug (developer) builds never send anything.

Retention and deletion

Google Analytics keeps event data for no longer than 14 months; Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. To ask us to delete data associated with your installation, email zhanghang2357@gmail.com.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Data stored on your device

  • Click settings (interval, repeat count, jitter, playback timing), the current list of actions (tap/swipe positions and timing), your saved scripts and the usage-data switch are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your in-app consent to the accessibility disclosure and your chosen app language are stored locally.
  • This locally stored data itself is not uploaded. Only the anonymous usage events described above (which may mention, for example, which setting was changed or the chosen app language) are sent, and only while usage data sharing is on.

Backup

If Android backup (for example Google backup) is turned on for your device, your settings, actions and scripts may be included in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account and is end-to-end encrypted where supported by your device. We have no access to it. The accessibility consent record and the Firebase identifiers are excluded from backup.

Retention and deletion of local data

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete scripts in the app. Clearing the app's storage (Settings › Apps › Auto Clicker › Storage › Clear storage) or uninstalling the app removes all of its local data, including the Firebase installation ID.

Security

Local data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Usage data and crash reports are transmitted over encrypted connections.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for a general adult audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address. If the way the app uses the Accessibility Service changes materially, the app will ask for your consent again.

Contact

Questions about this policy or data requests: zhanghang2357@gmail.com.

概要

  • 没有账号、没有广告。我们不收集个人信息，也绝不出售数据或将其用于广告。
  • 你的点击、滑动、录制和方案都只保存在你的设备上；无障碍服务经手的任何信息都不会被收集或上传。

无障碍服务（AccessibilityService API）

本应用是自动化工具，不是无障碍辅助工具。它使用 Android 无障碍服务 API，只用来执行你自己设置的点击和滑动：

  • 执行手势：在你摆放或录制的位置点击、滑动，而且只在你点了悬浮条上的播放之后。
  • 录制：只在录制期间，记录你在屏幕上触摸的位置和时长（坐标和时间），用来原样回放。这些信息作为方案的一部分保存在你的设备上，不会上传。
  • 不读取屏幕：服务没有开启 canRetrieveWindowContent，也不订阅无障碍事件，读不到屏幕上的文字、密码、消息或其他应用的内容。

跳转到系统无障碍设置之前，本应用会先在应用内显示用途说明并请你同意。你不同意时，应用的其它功能（编辑方案和设置）照常可用。你可以随时在系统无障碍设置里关闭本服务。

显示在其他应用上层（SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW）

只用来在其他应用上层显示悬浮控制条、编号目标标记和录制层。

匿名使用数据和崩溃报告（Firebase）

本应用使用 Google 提供的 Firebase Analytics 和 Firebase Crashlytics，Google 作为我们的服务提供方处理这些数据。

收集什么

  • 应用使用事件，例如：显示了悬浮条、开始或停止播放、保存了录制、保存/选择/删除了方案、切换了语言或修改了某项设置、授予了权限。事件里只有分段后的数量（如「2-5 个动作」）、分段后的时长和固定选项（如点击/滑动、停止原因、被修改的设置项名称、所选语言）。所选语言也作为用户属性记录。
  • 崩溃日志（崩溃时的调用栈和应用状态）和诊断信息。
  • 设备信息：设备型号、Android 版本、应用版本、语言/地区设置。
  • 大致位置：由 Google Analytics 根据你的 IP 地址推断出的国家/地区和城市。本应用不申请定位权限，绝不收集精确（GPS）位置；IP 地址仅用于推断大致位置，Google Analytics 不会保存 IP 地址本身。
  • Firebase 安装 ID：为本次安装随机生成的标识，不与你的姓名、邮箱或 Google 账号关联；清除应用存储或重新安装后会重置。

绝不收集

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话、账号等个人信息。
  • 精确（GPS）位置。
  • 无障碍服务的任何内容：屏幕内容、文字、点击或滑动的位置、手势路径。
  • 你使用或自动操作的其他应用（包名），以及你给方案起的名字。
  • 广告 ID（应用已移除 AD_ID 权限，并关闭了广告 ID、SSAID 采集和广告个性化信号）。

用途

只用于了解功能使用情况、改进应用、发现和修复崩溃。数据不出售、不用于广告，除作为服务提供方的 Google 外不与任何人分享；传输全程加密（HTTPS）。

你的选择

「共享匿名使用数据」默认开启，可随时在 设置 › 隐私 中关闭。关闭后，本应用既不发送使用事件，也不发送崩溃报告。调试（开发者）版本任何数据都不发送。

保留与删除

Google Analytics 的事件数据最长保留 14 个月；Crashlytics 的崩溃报告保留 90 天。如需删除与你的安装相关的数据，请发邮件至 zhanghang2357@gmail.com。

Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy · Google 如何使用来自其服务合作方应用的数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

保存在设备上的数据

  • 点击参数（间隔、轮数、抖动、按录制节奏）、当前动作列表（点击/滑动的位置和时长）、你保存的方案以及「共享匿名使用数据」开关，存放在本应用的私有存储中。

备份

如果你的设备开启了 Android 备份（例如 Google 备份），参数、动作和方案可能包含在你自己的设备备份中，由 Google 在你的 Google 账号下处理（设备支持时为端到端加密）。我们无法访问。无障碍同意记录和 Firebase 标识不参与备份。

本机数据的保留与删除

本机数据会一直保存在你的设备上，直到你删除。你可以在应用内删除方案；清除应用存储（设置 › 应用 › 自动点击 › 存储 › 清除存储）或卸载应用会删除它的全部本机数据，包括 Firebase 安装 ID。

安全

本机数据保存在本应用的私有存储中，其他应用无法读取。使用数据和崩溃报告通过加密连接传输。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，面向一般成年用户。
